Master's in Germany 2027: A Complete Guide for Indian Students

Admissions, eligibility, APS certification, dMAT, tuition fees and student visas

Planning a master's degree in Germany for 2027 involves choosing the right university, understanding admission requirements and preparing documents on time. Germany study abroad consultants in Kerala can help students navigate course selections, application procedures and important deadlines.

For students exploring study abroad consultants in Kerala, understanding the admission process is equally important. This guide covers the 2027 intake options, eligibility requirements, APS certification, the new dMAT rules, tuition fees, visa procedures and opportunities after graduation.

Typically, a master's degree in Germany can be completed within two to four semesters, depending on the programme and institution. Students can choose between research-oriented universities and universities of applied sciences that prioritize practical experience.

Popular postgraduate fields include mechanical engineering, automotive engineering, computer science, artificial intelligence, data science, renewable energy and business management.

Another advantage of studying in Germany is that higher education can be comparatively affordable. Many public universities do not charge tuition fees for standard master's programmes, although exceptions and additional charges apply.

What Are the Master's Intake Options in Germany for 2027?

Students intending to pursue a master's degree in Germany in 2027 need to be aware of the two key application periods.

Summer Intake 2027

The summer semester generally begins in March or April. Applications for many programmes close around January 15, 2027, although some universities have earlier deadlines.

Winter Intake 2027

The winter semester typically starts in September or October. July 15 is a common application deadline at some institutions, but master's programmes may follow different schedules.

Applicants should check each university's official website for programme-specific deadlines. As application processing times vary, it is wise to prepare and submit documents well before the deadline.

Eligibility Requirements for Indian Students

German universities assess applicants' previous academic achievements alongside the requirements of their chosen programmes.

Important requirements generally include:

  • Recognized bachelor's degree: Applicants need a recognized undergraduate qualification. Some programmes require a degree in a specific or related field.
  • Academic performance: Minimum grades vary by university and course. Some programmes also require particular subjects or a specified number of academic credits.
  • Language proficiency: English-taught courses may accept IELTS or TOEFL scores. German-taught courses normally require proof of German-language proficiency.
  • Additional evaluations: Certain programmes may request GRE or GMAT scores, portfolios or other subject-specific assessments.

Students with a three-year bachelor's degree from India should verify their eligibility for each programme, as requirements vary according to the qualification, university and course.

APS Certificate and New dMAT Requirements for 2027

The Academic Evaluation Centre (APS India) verifies academic documents from Indian educational institutions. An APS certificate is required for many Indian applicants, subject to the applicable exemptions.

One important change to investigate for 2027 is the Digital Master Application Test (dMAT).

According to the information provided for this guide, dMAT may apply to selected master's applicants with academic backgrounds in engineering, commerce, accounting, finance, economics, business or management.

The stated requirement applies from the summer semester 2027 onwards. Applicants who registered for APS before June 29, 2026, submitted complete documents before that date or had already completed their APS procedure may qualify for exemptions, depending on the official rules.

The test does not replace APS verification or guarantee university admission. Students should verify the official dMAT requirements, affected fields and exemption rules before applying.

Cost of Studying in Germany in 2027

Understanding both tuition fees and living expenses is essential when planning postgraduate education in Germany.

Tuition Fees

Many German public universities do not charge tuition fees for standard master's programmes. However, students generally need to pay a semester contribution. DAAD reports that these contributions can vary by institution.

Important exceptions include:

  • Baden-Württemberg: Eligible non-EU students generally pay tuition fees of €1,500 per semester, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Technical University of Munich: Many master's programmes charge tuition fees to non-EU students. Fees vary by programme.
  • Private universities: Tuition fees are programme-specific and can be considerably higher than those at public universities.

Living Expenses and Blocked Account

Living costs depend on accommodation, city, insurance, transport and personal spending habits. Students should prepare a realistic monthly budget before selecting a university.

The financial proof required for a German student visa may involve a blocked account. The required amount can change, so applicants should check the latest official visa guidance before transferring money.

How to Apply for Master's Programmes in Germany

The application process usually begins with selecting suitable courses through university websites and study-programme databases such as DAAD.

Applicants generally need to prepare the following documents:

  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Language proficiency certificates
  • APS certificate, where required
  • Curriculum vitae (CV or resume)
  • Statement of purpose or motivation letter
  • Letters of recommendation, if required
  • GRE, GMAT or other test results, if required

Applications may be submitted directly to universities or through uni-assist. Some universities require a preliminary review documentation certificate known as a VPD.

Applicants should carefully follow document formats, certification instructions and application procedures. Incomplete applications may cause delays, particularly when academic verification or additional assessments are necessary.

German Student Visa Requirements for Indian Applicants

After obtaining admission, students can begin the student visa process using the official German Consular Services Portal and the instructions applicable to their location.

Typical requirements may include:

  • A valid passport
  • University admission letter
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • APS certificate, where required
  • Language test results, where applicable
  • Proof of sufficient financial resources
  • Health insurance documentation
  • Additional documents requested by the visa authorities

Requirements can change, and the German Embassy or responsible consulate may request further documents. Avoid making non-refundable travel arrangements before receiving your visa decision.

Scholarships and Opportunities After Graduation

Indian students can explore funding opportunities through DAAD scholarships, the Deutschlandstipendium and university-specific scholarship programmes. Eligibility criteria, award amounts and deadlines differ.

International students with eligible residence permits can generally work up to 140 full days or 280 half-days per calendar year under the applicable rules. Students should confirm the conditions attached to their residence permit.

After successfully completing a German degree, eligible non-EU graduates can apply for a residence permit of up to 18 months to look for qualified employment.

Learning German while studying in an English-taught programme can also help students communicate during internships, job applications and everyday life.

When Should Students Start Preparing for the 2027 Intake?

Students considering the summer 2027 intake should focus on application deadlines, academic verification and programme-specific requirements as early as possible.

For the winter 2027 intake, preparation can begin in late 2026, giving applicants time to shortlist universities, take language exams, arrange APS documentation and meet individual programme requirements.

Germany offers Indian graduates opportunities to pursue specialized education across different fee structures, study formats and career pathways.

When planning for admission in 2027, students should choose suitable academic programmes, check their APS and dMAT eligibility, prepare a realistic budget and submit complete applications before the relevant university deadlines.
