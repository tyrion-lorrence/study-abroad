Masters in Germany for Indian Students: Guide for 2027 Intake

Planning a master's degree in Germany for 2027 involves choosing the right university, understanding admission requirements and preparing documents on time. Germany study abroad consultants in Kerala can help students navigate course selections, application procedures and important deadlines.

For students exploring Study abroad consultants in Kerala, understanding the admission process is equally important. This guide covers the 2027 intake options, eligibility requirements, APS certification, the new dMAT rules, tuition fees, visa procedures and opportunities after graduation.

Typically, a Master’s degree in Germany can be acquired within two to four semesters, based on the program and the institution. There is an option for choosing between research-oriented universities and technical colleges that prioritize hands-on experience.

Popular postgraduate fields include mechanical engineering, automotive engineering, computer science, artificial intelligence, data science, renewable energy and business management.

Another advantage Germany has is that higher education there is comparatively cheap. In fact, many state universities in Germany do not collect any tuition fees from students enrolled in regular masters programs. The only exception to this rule exists.

What Are the Master's Intake Options in Germany for 2027?

Students intending to pursue a Masters degree in Germany in 2027 need to be aware of the two key application periods.

Summer Intake 2027

The summer semester generally begins in March or April. Applications for many programmes close around January 15, 2027, although some universities have earlier deadlines.

Winter Intake 2027

Winter semester typically starts in September or October. July 15 can be seen as a general date for application deadlines, although master’s programs usually operate on a different schedule.

As stated by uni-assist, students are advised to apply no later than eight weeks before the deadline. It is essential that students check the deadlines for the universities of their choice.

Eligibility Requirements for Indian Students

The German universities consider the past achievements of the postgraduate candidates in addition to the criteria that are required by the chosen program.

Important requirements generally include:

  • Recognized bachelor’s degree: It is mandatory for candidates to have a recognized undergraduate degree from the university. Some programs will necessitate a relevant background.
  • Academic performance: Different requirements exist for minimum grades per university and course. In addition, certain programs have prerequisite subjects/credits.
  • Language proficiency: English-based courses usually accept English language tests, which include IELTS or TOEFL. German-language based courses normally need a certificate proving their proficiency in the language.
  • Further evaluations: Some programs could ask for GRE, GMAT, portfolios, or other discipline-specific tests.

Those students holding a three-year bachelor’s degree from India must check whether they have the eligibility for that specific program, since eligibility varies according to the degree and the university.

APS Certificate and New dMAT Requirements for 2027

The Academic Verification Centre (APS India) checks academic certificates from Indian institutes. The APS certificate is always needed for Indian students, but some exceptions apply.

One of the most important changes that should be noted for the year 2027 is the implementation of the Digital Master Test (dMAT).

According to APS India, dMAT will apply to a selected few master's applicants who have a degree in engineering, commerce, accounting, finance, economics, business or management.

The requirement applies from the summer semester 2027 onwards. Applicants who registered for APS before June 29, 2026, submitted complete documents before that date or already completed their APS procedure may qualify for exemptions.

The test does not replace APS verification or guarantee university admission. Students should review the official affected-fields list and exemption rules before beginning their applications.

Cost of Studying in Germany in 2027

It is crucial to know about both tuition fees and living costs for postgraduate education.

Tuition Fees

Many German public universities do not charge tuition for standard master's degrees. However, students must pay semester contributions, which DAAD places at approximately €70–€430 per semester.

Important exceptions include:

  • Baden-Württemberg: Generally charges eligible non-EU students €1,500 per semester.
  • Technical University of Munich: Many master's programmes charge non-EU students approximately €4,000–€6,000 per semester.
  • Private universities: Charge programme-specific tuition fees, which can be considerably higher.

Living Expenses and Blocked Account

DAAD estimates typical student living expenses at approximately €900–€1,200 monthly, depending on accommodation and location.

In 2026, for Germany, the cost of the student visa will be €11,904 for the year and €992 per month. It may differ in 2027, so it is essential to verify the appropriate figure before making the payment.

How to Apply for Master's Programmes in Germany

The application process usually starts with selecting the courses either through DAAD or the university websites.

Once the courses have been selected, the student needs to prepare all documents including transcript of records, degree certificate, language proficiency certificates, APS certification, resume and the statement of purpose/recommendation letters.

The application could be sent to the universities directly or using uni-assist. There are some universities that will ask for preliminary documentation which is referred to as VPD.

Applicants need to pay close attention to document formats, certificates and application procedures. Incomplete applications might lead to delays, especially where academic verification or further assessment is necessary.

German Student Visa Requirements for Indian Applicants

Once they obtain admission, students can apply for their visa via the German Consular Services Portal.

Typical requirements include a passport, admission letter, academic transcript, relevant APS certificate, language test results, financial capability and health insurance documentation.

The German Embassy of India may ask for more documents from time to time.

It is recommended not to make any non-reversible travel arrangements until you receive your visa decision.

Scholarships and Opportunities After Graduation

Indian students can explore financial assistance through DAAD scholarships, the Deutschlandstipendium and university-specific funding programmes. Eligibility, award amounts and application deadlines vary.

International students holding eligible study residence permits can generally work up to 140 full days or 280 half-days annually, subject to applicable rules.

After successfully completing a German degree, eligible non-EU graduates can apply for a residence permit allowing up to 18 months to seek qualified employment.

Acquiring German while studying for a course taught in English can also enhance communication during internships, job applications, and day-to-day life.
