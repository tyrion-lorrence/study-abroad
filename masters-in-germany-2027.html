Master's in Germany 2027 for Indian Students: Intakes, APS, dMAT, Fees and Visa

Planning a master's degree in Germany for 2027 involves choosing the right university, understanding admission requirements and preparing documents on time. This guide covers the main steps Indian students should consider.

Germany is a popular destination for postgraduate study because it offers a broad range of research-oriented universities and technical institutions. Many public universities charge no tuition fees for standard master's programmes, although semester contributions and some programme-specific or state-level fees may apply. Admission requirements, costs and deadlines vary by institution and course, so always confirm details on official university websites.

Important: Requirements and fees can change. Verify current deadlines, APS procedures, dMAT rules, visa financial requirements and programme-specific criteria with the relevant official authorities before applying.

Master's Intake Options in Germany for 2027

Students planning to pursue a master's degree in Germany in 2027 should pay attention to the two main application periods.

Summer intake 2027

The summer semester generally begins in March or April. Applications for many programmes close around January 15, 2027, although some universities set earlier or later deadlines. Not every programme offers a summer intake, so check the course page carefully.

Winter intake 2027

The winter semester typically starts in September or October. July 15 is a common application deadline, but deadlines vary by university and programme. Some master's programmes close applications months earlier. As a general planning guide, begin preparing well in advance and aim to submit a complete application at least eight weeks before the relevant deadline where possible.

Eligibility Requirements for Indian Students

German universities assess applicants according to their previous academic achievements and the entry criteria for the selected programme. Common requirements include:

  • Recognised bachelor's degree: Applicants generally need a recognised undergraduate degree that meets the programme's academic requirements. Some courses require a closely related field of study.
  • Academic performance: Minimum grades and grade-conversion rules vary by university and course. Some programmes also require specific subjects or credits.
  • Language proficiency: English-taught courses may accept IELTS or TOEFL scores. German-taught courses usually require proof of German-language proficiency. Accepted tests and minimum scores differ by programme.
  • Additional assessments: Depending on the course, applicants may need GRE or GMAT scores, a portfolio, work experience, recommendation letters or other discipline-specific documents.

APS Certificate and New dMAT Requirements for 2027

The Academic Evaluation Centre (APS India) verifies academic documents for applicants who studied in India. An APS certificate is required for many Indian students applying to German universities, subject to the current rules and any applicable exemptions. Check the official APS India website and the university's instructions before applying.

The Digital Master Test (dMAT) may apply to selected master's applicants under the rules in force for the relevant intake. Its applicability depends on the programme and the applicant's circumstances. Do not assume that every applicant must take the test—or that a test exemption applies—without checking the latest official guidance.

Applicants should review the current affected-field list, document requirements, deadlines and exemption criteria. If you believe you qualify for an exemption, confirm it with the responsible authority or university before relying on it.

Cost of Studying in Germany in 2027

Plan for both education-related charges and everyday living costs. The total depends on the city, accommodation, university and programme.

Tuition fees and semester contributions

Many public universities do not charge tuition for standard master's programmes, but students generally pay a semester contribution. Important exceptions may include:

  • Baden-Württemberg: Eligible non-EU students may be charged tuition of around €1,500 per semester, subject to exemptions and current state rules.
  • Technical University of Munich: Some master's programmes charge tuition fees for students from outside the EU/EEA. The amount varies by programme and should be checked on the university's official website.
  • Private universities: These commonly charge programme-specific tuition fees, which can be substantially higher than semester contributions at public universities.

Even where tuition is not charged, budget for semester contributions, accommodation, food, transport, health insurance, study materials and personal expenses.

Living expenses and blocked account

Living costs vary by location and lifestyle. As a rough planning estimate, students may need approximately €900–€1,200 per month, but actual expenses can be higher in expensive cities. Visa applicants may need to demonstrate sufficient financial resources through a blocked account or another accepted method. The required amount changes over time; check the German mission's current instructions for the year you apply.

How to Apply for Master's Programmes in Germany

  1. Shortlist courses: Identify programmes that match your academic background, interests, language skills and career goals. Check each course's entry requirements and intake dates.
  2. Check the application route: Applications may be submitted directly to the university or through uni-assist. Some institutions use uni-assist for a preliminary review or a VPD (preliminary review documentation). Follow the process specified by each university.
  3. Prepare your documents: Requirements commonly include a passport, academic transcripts, degree certificate, CV, motivation letter, language-test results and recommendation letters. APS documentation may also be required.
  4. Submit before the deadline: Follow file-format, translation, certification and submission instructions. Incomplete applications can be delayed or rejected.
  5. Track your application: Monitor the university portal and email for requests, interview invitations and admission decisions.

German Student Visa Requirements for Indian Applicants

After receiving admission, check the current student-visa procedure with the German mission or its authorised service provider in India. Depending on your circumstances, documents may include:

  • A valid passport and completed visa application.
  • University admission letter and academic documents.
  • Proof of financial resources in an accepted form.
  • Health-insurance documentation, as required.
  • Language-test results and any additional documents requested by the visa authority.

Requirements can change and additional documents may be requested. Avoid making non-refundable travel arrangements until your visa has been granted.

Scholarships and Opportunities After Graduation

Indian students can investigate DAAD scholarships, Deutschlandstipendium and funding offered by individual universities or external organisations. Eligibility, award amounts and deadlines vary, so check each scholarship's official criteria and apply early.

After successfully completing a German degree, eligible international graduates may be able to apply for a residence permit to seek qualified employment. The duration and conditions depend on the rules in force at the time. Students should confirm the current requirements with the relevant immigration authority.

When Should You Start Preparing for the 2027 Intake?

Start as early as practical—ideally around a year before your intended start date. Early preparation gives you time to shortlist universities, take language tests, gather transcripts, complete APS procedures, check whether dMAT applies, prepare applications and arrange finances.

For a 2027 intake, make a schedule for each university rather than relying on one general deadline. Keep copies of submitted documents and check official sources regularly for updates.

Final Checklist

  • Choose suitable universities and programmes.
  • Confirm the intake and exact application deadline for every course.
  • Check academic, language and programme-specific eligibility.
  • Verify current APS and dMAT requirements and any exemptions.
  • Prepare application documents and submit them in the required format.
  • Budget for tuition (if applicable), semester contributions and living expenses.
  • Check current visa and financial-proof requirements before applying.
